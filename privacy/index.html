Privacy policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

The short version: KnowMyStory collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your Story Passport is saved only on your device, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us or anyone else.

Who we are

KnowMyStory is published by Keviz Design and Consultancy LLP ("we", "us"). This policy covers the KnowMyStory app for iPhone and iPad.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. It does not use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking services, and it does not use your device's advertising identifier.

What stays on your device

  • Your Story Passport. The stamps and points you earn are stored locally on your device. We cannot see them, and they are not backed up to our servers because we don't run any.
  • Stories, art and fonts. These are built into the app, so stories play offline and the app doesn't need to download them.

To erase your progress, delete the app. That removes everything it has stored.

Links to other websites

Some screens list sources and places to visit, with links to outside websites such as tourism boards, news sites, Wikipedia or booking pages. When you tap one, it opens in your web browser, outside the app. Those websites are run by others and have their own privacy policies, which govern anything you do there. We don't control them and aren't responsible for how they handle your data.

Sharing a stamp

If you choose to share a stamp, the app opens your device's standard share sheet. You decide where it goes, and the app sends nothing by itself.

Photos and credits

Photos in the stories are freely licensed or public domain images, mostly from Wikimedia Commons, and each one is credited in the app. They are bundled with the app, so viewing them sends no data.

Children

KnowMyStory is made for a general audience. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding accounts or ads, we will update this page and the effective date before that version is released, and describe the change in the App Store release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email contact@keviz.in.
