KnowMyStory lets you play the story of a real place before you go. Each story takes about 7 minutes: explore it in 3D, make a choice, play a quick game, see the place today, then earn a stamp for your Story Passport.

Questions people ask

How do stamps work?

Every story ends with a memory quest: a few quick questions about what you just played. Finish it and you earn that story's stamp, plus points for what you discovered along the way. Open your Story Passport from the home screen to see every stamp you've collected.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account, ever. You need a connection the first time you play each story, while it downloads (about 1 MB). After that it plays offline, so you can play on a plane or anywhere without signal. Download the stories you want before you travel. New stories appear on their own, no app update needed.

How do I reset my progress?

Your passport is saved only on your device, so there's no account to reset. To start fresh, delete the app and install it again. That clears every stamp, point and downloaded story.

Heads up: this also means stamps don't move to a new phone or iPad.

Where do the photos come from?

The real photos are freely licensed or public domain images, mostly from Wikimedia Commons. Each one shows its author and licence right where it appears in the story.

Are the stories true?

The places, dates, numbers and real people are, and every story lists its sources on its "place today" screen. Some characters, like the kid you play, are made up to walk you through it.

I spotted a fact that looks wrong. What do I do?

Tell us. Email contact@keviz.in with the story name, the line you mean, and a source if you have one. We check every report and fix it in the next version.

Can you make a story about my city?

We'd love to hear it. Email contact@keviz.in with the place, person or brand, and the one detail that made you want to know more.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad.
